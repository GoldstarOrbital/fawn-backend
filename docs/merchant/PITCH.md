## Stop paying 2.5% to accept a payment. Pay a penny.

**FAWN is a payment network built so a $60 sale costs you $0.01 — not $1.75.**

### The problem you already know

You're banking-restricted, so you run on cash and workarounds:

- **Cash** means theft risk, armored-car fees, counting labor, and a safe.
- **"Cashless ATM"** hits your customer with $3–$3.50 and misrepresents the
  transaction to the debit networks — the practice regulators cracked down on.
- **Pay-by-bank (Aeropay et al.)** is legitimate, but costs **2.5% + $0.25**.
  On a $60 basket that's **$1.75 out of your margin, every time.**

### What FAWN costs

| | On a $60 basket |
|---|---|
| Aeropay-style pay-by-bank | **$1.75** |
| Cashless ATM | **$3.00–$3.50** (customer pays) |
| **FAWN** | **$0.01** |

**That is ~175× cheaper.** At 150 transactions a day, pay-by-bank costs you
about **$95,000/year**. FAWN costs about **$550/year.**

### How it works

1. Customer holds a USDC balance in the FAWN app (a real, dollar-backed
   stablecoin — $1 stays $1).
2. At your register they **tap their phone** or scan a QR.
3. Funds land in **your** FAWN balance **instantly** — final, no chargebacks.
4. Hold it as working capital, or withdraw on-chain to a wallet you control.

**No card networks. No interchange. No chargebacks. No 1–3 day settlement.**

### What you need

- A state cannabis license in good standing (we verify it — and re-verify it).
- **Your existing bank account.** FAWN feeds the banking relationship you
  already have; we don't replace it and we don't provide it.
- 30 minutes of your web/POS developer's time (one `<script>` tag).

### What FAWN is straight with you about

- **We are not a bank** and we don't provide cannabis banking. If you need to
  turn USDC into dollars, that runs through your own MRB-friendly bank.
- **Your customers need the FAWN app and a funded balance** before they reach
  the register. We do that onboarding work with you — on campus, in-store
  signage, and at your counter.
- **No chargebacks cuts both ways:** payments are final, so refunds are your
  process, not a dispute system's.

### Pilot terms

- $0.01 per transaction. No monthly fee. No setup fee. No minimum.
- No exclusivity — run FAWN alongside whatever you use today.
- Cancel anytime.

# PART 2 — Acquisition playbook (internal)

## The PayPal cold-start lesson

PayPal didn't launch "payments for the internet." It launched *payments for
eBay power sellers* — one dense cluster with acute pain and repeat frequency,
then expanded. Translate directly:

| PayPal | FAWN |
|---|---|
| eBay power sellers | **Bay Area dispensaries** |
| Sellers had no good way to take money | Dispensaries are banking-restricted |
| High frequency, repeat buyers | 100–300 transactions/day, regulars |
| Seed the buyer side with $10 | Seed the customer side via **campus** |

**FAWN's unfair advantage:** you already have a consumer beachhead being
built — Bay Area college students launching Aug 13. Students are (a) a real
dispensary demographic where legal, (b) already the target of your campus
signup drive, and (c) exactly the cohort that will adopt a phone-tap payment.
**One acquisition motion feeds both sides of the marketplace.** That is a
genuinely defensible cold-start story for investors.

## Market size (verified)

- **~900 dispensaries in California**, **200+ in the Bay Area** — dense enough
  to walk door-to-door, which is the whole point.

## Qualifying questions (ask these first — they kill bad leads fast)

1. **"Who do you bank with today?"** → Must have an MRB-friendly bank/credit
   union. *No bank = they can't get USD out = deprioritize.*
2. "What do you pay to accept non-cash payments now?" → Sizes the pain.
3. "Roughly how many transactions a day?" → Sizes the revenue.
4. "Is your state license current, and when does it renew?" → Hard gate.
5. "Who builds/maintains your website or POS?" → Names the integrator.

## Outreach sequence

**Week 1 — 5 design partners.** Walk in mid-afternoon (post-lunch lull, before
evening rush). Ask for the owner/GM. Lead with the number:
> *"You're paying about $1.75 every time someone pays by bank. We do it for a
> penny. I'm local, I built it, and I'll set it up myself for free."*

**Week 2 — Integrate 1–2.** Do the integration *for* them, on-site. Bring the
`<script>` tag and the test key. Target: live in one afternoon.

**Week 3–4 — Prove it.** Get 50+ real transactions through one store. Capture:
transactions, dollars saved vs. their current processor, tap-to-confirm time.

**Week 5+ — Referral.** Dispensary owners talk to each other constantly. A
Tier-1 operator with 3 stores and a real savings number is the whole Series-A
case study.

## What to measure

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Dispensaries live | Merchant-side traction |
| Transactions/store/day | Frequency proves habit, not novelty |
| **$ saved vs. incumbent** | The only number that closes the next merchant |
| Student wallets funded near a store | Two-sided flywheel health |
| Tap→confirm seconds | Beats cash at the register or it doesn't get used |

## The objection you will get, and the honest answer

> *"My customers don't have USDC. How does this help me today?"*

Don't dodge it. **This is true and it's the hardest part.** The answer:
FAWN seeds the customer side through the campus launch and in-store signup,
and the dispensary pays **nothing** to have FAWN available as an option
alongside what they already run. Zero cost, zero exclusivity, zero downside —
they only win if it works. Anyone promising instant customer volume is lying,
and dispensary owners have heard enough of that.
